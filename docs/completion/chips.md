# Chips (criterion C2)

Generated by `patch/tools/inventory.py` (see [method.md](method.md)). Ids are chip ids (`0x000`–`0x184`). "Library number" is the record's `+0x1C` field: standard 1–150, secret 151–186, Mega 200+, Giga 280+, PA 300+.

## 1. The chip table is the same in both versions

All 389 records (`0x080197EC`, `0x2C` bytes each) are byte-identical between Red Sun and Blue Moon. So the merged ROM doesn't need the other version's chip *data*. It needs the other version's **sources**: battles, drops and traders. For Navi chips, it also needs the Navi's **battle**, whose AI and graphics are version data (DC-4).

## 2. Obtainable in one version only (36 + 10)

**Navi chips (Mega, 18 per version):** each Soul Navi's chip, its SP version and its DS version.

| Red Sun | Blue Moon |
|---|---|
| Roll, RollSP, RollDS | ProtoMan, ProtoMSP, ProtoMDS |
| GutsMan, GutsMnSP, GutsMnDS | NumbrMan, NumbMnSP, NumbMnDS |
| WindMan, WindMnSP, WindMnDS | MetalMan, MetlMnSP, MetlMnDS |
| SerchMan, SrchMnSP, SrchMnDS | JunkMan, JunkMnSP, JunkMnDS |
| FireMan, FireMnSP, FireMnDS | AquaMan, AquaMnSP, AquaMnDS |
| ThunMan, ThunMnSP, ThunMnDS | WoodMan, WoodMnSP, WoodMnDS |

They come from beating the Navi's V2, V3 and SP versions, from an S rank against SP, and from the DS version in Black Earth 2. In the original game, a traded copy shows up in the other version's Library as a secret chip.

**Giga chips (5 per version, can't be traded):**

| Red Sun | Blue Moon |
|---|---|
| Bass, RedSun, HolyDrem, BlakBarr, BugCharg | BassAnly, BlueMoon, DeltaRay, BugCurse, SignlRed |

Sources include story rewards, the Bass fight in Black Earth 2, S ranks against SP Navis, BugFrag traders, and in-battle Green Mystery Data against SP Navis.

## 4. To check against the ROM

- **Codes with no listed source:** WideSht3 U, Vulcan3 T, TwnFng3 H, MokoRus2 U, DublCrak B/L/R, TripCrak B/L/R. The record has the code, but the reference gives no source. They may come from Green Mystery Data or virus drops the guide missed, so check them against the ROM's GMD and drop tables.
- **LongSwrd and FstGauge:** not matched to the guide by name or number. The maps list LongSwrd from Blue Mystery Data (Electric Tower 2) and FstGauge in an Undernet 1 shop.
- **MetlGer3, BurnMnDS, FinalGun:** no source listed anywhere. FinalGun has two records (`0x122` Giga and `0x182`) and may be a cut chip.
- **Dark chips `0x0C3` and `0x0C4`:** no name and no codes; probably unused.

## 5. Not collectibles

- **30 Program Advances:** combinations of chips the player already has. A PA is counted once it has been used (the Library flag table at `0x0801F508`, written by `sub_0801F4E4`: see [`../re/save-and-flags.md`](../re/save-and-flags.md)).
- **57 internal records:** Soul charge attacks (FirePlus, ThunPlus, AquaPowr, WoodPowr, BlakWeap), MegaMan's modes (CannMode, SwrdMode, Marking), the Soul and Dark forms, debug entries (FastFwd, Rewind, Training) and empty records.
