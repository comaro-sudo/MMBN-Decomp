## Key Decisions & Constraints

- **Validation Result (`5,771 / 5,802 = 99.47%` pass rate):**
  - **5,771 pointer pairs pass** `BM == map(RS)` (`bm_start + (RS - rs_start)`).
  - **All 31 non-matching pairs are completely explained:**
    1. **21 palette-row selector pointers in `code_02` (`0x0802F7D8`–`0x0802F968`, every `0x14` bytes):** Point into the *byte-identical* shared map/object palette archive at RS `0x086F7ACC`–`0x086FA8AC` (which sits at a constant `-0x50C` delta in BM), where BM deliberately selects a different 16-color palette row (`+0x20`, `+0x40`, `+0x60`, `+0x80`, `+0xE0`, `+0x140`, or `+0x200` bytes higher) for version-colored objects. Keeping the underlying palette archive at `-0x50C` allows `disasm.py` to symbolize the BM pointer as `dat_<RS_base> + <row_offset>` or keep the 21 descriptor entries in `.if VERSION_BM`.
    2. **1 intentional cross-region save-init pointer in `code_00` (`0x08006334`):** RS loads `0x08126511`, BM loads `0x082F123C`.
    3. **9 non-pointer 4-byte constant sequences inside `incrom` tables:** Bytecode/table constants in `code_01` (`0x08020021`), `code_04` (`0x08066D51`), `code_05` (`0x080730B3`), and `code_09` (`0x080F7130`–`0x080F8353`) whose 4th byte happens to be `0x08` or `0x88`.
- **Swapped 80-byte LZ77 block at `0x086E3CFC`:**
  - In RS, the `0x50`-byte LZ77 asset at `0x086E3CFC`–`0x086E3D4C` precedes the `0x510`-byte LZ77 asset at `0x086E3D4C`–`0x086E425C`; in BM their order is swapped (`0x086E37F0`–`0x086E3D00` followed by `0x086E3D00`–`0x086E3D50`). Recording `086E3CFC 086E3D4C 086E3D00 same` (`delta = +0x4`) and `086E3D4C 086E425C 086E37F0 same` (`delta = -0x55C`) maps both assets and their code-region pointers (`code_02` `0x0803356C` and `code_03` `0x080408F4`) with 100% accuracy.
