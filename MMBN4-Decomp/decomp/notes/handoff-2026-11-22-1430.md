## Key Decisions & Constraints
- **`0x0800..0x083F` (`lsrs Rd, Rm, #0x20`) as a ROM Pointer / Data Signature:** In GBA ROMs, every 32-bit pointer into `0x08000000..0x083FFFFF` has upper halfword `0x0800..0x083F`, which disassembles in Thumb as `lsrs Rd, Rm, #0x20`. Furthermore, pointers to functions at `0x08XXB5YY` have lower halfword `0xB5YY` (`push {..., lr}`). Rejecting `(hw & 0xFFC0) == 0x0800` prevents 32-bit ROM pointers and action script headers from ever being misclassified as Thumb code.
